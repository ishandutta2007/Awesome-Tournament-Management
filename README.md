# 🏆 Awesome-Tournament-Management

## 🌟 Top Tournament Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on ⚽ Sports & 🎮 Esports Tournament Organization, Brackets, Scheduling, Registration, Scoring & Event Management*  
**Last updated: August 2026**

Welcome to the ultimate resource for tournament management! This SEO-friendly repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Tournament Management**. Whether you are organizing grassroots sports leagues, competitive esports tournaments, or recreational competitions, these tools will help you seamlessly create brackets, manage registrations, schedule matches, track live scores, and handle payments.

**Examples** include category leaders like LeagueApps, GotSport, Tourney Machine, SportsEngine, PlayHQ, BracketHQ, TeamSnap Tournaments, LeagueLobster, EventConnect, and Toornament.

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosted tournament systems, bracket generators, competition engines, and related open tools — ideal for clubs, leagues, esports organizers, and developers seeking free, customizable alternatives to commercial tournament platforms.

🤝 Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## 💼 SaaS/Hosted Platforms

| Platform | Description | Pricing | Free Tier Limit | Company Size (Revenue/Valuation) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[LeagueApps](https://leagueapps.com/)** | Comprehensive sports league and tournament management platform covering registration, scheduling, payments, communication, and event operations. | Starts with $495 one-time setup fee + 3.9% transaction fee | LeagueApps Play app is free; no free trial for main platform | ~$220M Valuation |
| **[SportsEngine](https://www.sportsengine.com/)** | Broad sports management suite from NBC Sports that includes tournament and league tools for registration, scheduling, and team communication. | Starts at $79/month (Express Plan) | Free trial available (duration varies based on custom needs) | ~$150M Valuation (Acquired) |
| **[TeamSnap Tournaments](https://www.teamsnap.com/)** | Tournament features within the TeamSnap ecosystem for team registration, scheduling, and event coordination. | Starts at $22.99/month per team (Premium plan) | 7-day free trial for new teams | ~$44.4M Revenue |
| **[GotSport](https://www.gotsport.com/)** | Soccer-focused tournament and competition management system with scheduling, referee assignment, brackets, and results tracking. | Starts at $4 per player/year | GotSport Live viewers get 2 months free; no free trial for main platform | ~$32M Valuation |
| **[PlayHQ](https://www.playhq.com/)** | Competition management platform widely used in Australia and beyond for fixtures, results, ladders, and club administration. | 1.99% + $0.30 transaction fee (custom scaling base fee) | Unlimited free trial for website builder until published | ~$13.5M Valuation |
| **[EventConnect](https://www.eventconnect.io/)** | Event and tournament management platform supporting registration, scheduling, and multi-event operations. | Starts at $27.99/month (Club Plan) | Free-to-start Lite plan for up to 50 attendees | ~$10M Revenue |
| **[Toornament](https://www.toornament.com/)** | Leading esports and competitive tournament platform with deep bracket, league, participant, and white-label capabilities. | Starts at 19€ per tournament (Boost Plan) | Free forever plan for up to 32 participants | ~$2.6M Valuation |
| **[Tourney Machine](https://www.tourneymachine.com/)** | Popular tournament operations platform specializing in brackets, live scoring, scheduling, and public event pages. | Starts at approximately $6 per team | Mobile app is free (ad-supported); no organizer free trial | Part of SportsEngine |
| **[LeagueLobster](https://www.leaguelobster.com/)** | Automated league and tournament scheduling software that generates fixtures and handles complex constraints. | Starts at $9.95/month | Free forever plan for up to 50 games per month | < $1M Revenue |
| **[BracketHQ](https://brackethq.com/)** | Modern tournament and bracket management tool focused on easy setup, live updates, and participant experience. | Starts at $4/month (Pro Plan) | Free forever plan for up to 32 participants | < $1M Revenue |

## 💻 Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[evroon/bracket](https://github.com/evroon/bracket)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/evroon/bracket?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/evroon/bracket/stargazers)  
  Feature-rich, self-hosted open-source tournament system supporting single elimination, round-robin, Swiss formats, multi-stage structures, scheduling, and public dashboards.

- **[brackets-manager/brackets-manager.js](https://github.com/brackets-manager/brackets-manager.js)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/brackets-manager/brackets-manager.js?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/brackets-manager/brackets-manager.js/stargazers)  
  A simple library to manage tournament brackets (single elimination, double elimination, round-robin).

- **[gitrgoliveira/bracket-creator](https://github.com/gitrgoliveira/bracket-creator)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/gitrgoliveira/bracket-creator?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/gitrgoliveira/bracket-creator/stargazers)  
  Open-source web app for creating and managing tournaments (with strong support for martial arts formats), including PDF/Excel export and real-time scoring.

- **[seattleowl/tourney](https://github.com/seattleowl/tourney)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/seattleowl/tourney?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/seattleowl/tourney/stargazers)  
  A lightweight, easy-to-use tournament bracket generator and manager for custom sports events.

- **[johnridesabike/coronate](https://github.com/johnridesabike/coronate)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/johnridesabike/coronate?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/johnridesabike/coronate/stargazers)  
  Free and open-source Swiss-system tournament manager originally built for chess, suitable for small clubs and events.

- **[CourtHive/tmx](https://github.com/CourtHive/tmx)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/CourtHive/tmx?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/CourtHive/tmx/stargazers)  
  Open-source tournament management platform (especially strong for tennis and racket sports) with draws, scheduling, scoring, live publishing, and TODS data standards support.

- **[CourtHive/competition-factory](https://github.com/CourtHive/competition-factory)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/CourtHive/competition-factory?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/CourtHive/competition-factory/stargazers)  
  Open-source multi-sport competition engine powering draws, scheduling, scoring, and rankings used by CourtHive tools.

### 📚 Additional Strong Open-Source Ecosystem Categories

- **Challonge API & Community Tools**  
  While Challonge itself is SaaS, numerous open-source clients, bots, and integrations leverage its API for custom tournament workflows.
- **Open Tournament Bracket Libraries**  
  Multiple JavaScript, Python, and other language libraries for generating and rendering single/double elimination, round-robin, and Swiss brackets.
- **Scoreboard & Live Results Tools**  
  Open-source display and overlay projects that pair with tournament backends for public-facing live scores and brackets.
